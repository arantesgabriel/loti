# Loti

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Users
A private group initially comprising five trusted people who keep favorites and buy together.

## Product Purpose
Replace the favorites and shared-purchase spreadsheet. Success is the complete favorite → purchase → HubBuy checklist → immutable history cycle.

## Positioning
Personal favorites, one collaborative purchase, no copying between sheets.

## Brand Personality
Warm, restrained, clear. Bookmark App provides structure; Clozy provides component polish.

## Anti-references
No analytics dashboard, ERP, glassmorphism, real product photos or public signup.

## Design Principles
Fast common paths; progressive disclosure; explicit ownership; independent historical snapshots; mobile-first behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Visible focus, keyboard accessible dialogs, text alongside state colors, comfortable touch targets and reduced motion.

Canonical details remain in docs/00_CONTEXT.md through docs/16_HUMAN_INPUTS.md. This file captures the approved brief for design tooling.
